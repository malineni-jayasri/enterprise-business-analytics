# BuildPro ERP Synthetic Dataset v1

A realistic synthetic ERP dataset for an end-to-end Data Analyst / Business Analyst portfolio project.

## Dataset scale

- Customers: 10,000 base customers + 100 controlled duplicate records
- Products: 3,000
- Orders: 100,000
- Order items: 300,000
- Suppliers: 250
- Warehouses: 40
- Sales representatives: 200
- Returns: 12,000
- Inventory rows: 120,000
- Pricing history rows: 45,000

## Embedded business patterns

- Seasonal construction demand
- Freight inflation beginning April 2025
- Regional freight differences
- Negotiated discounts for strategic customers
- Product popularity concentration
- West-region stockout concentration
- Returns connected to purchased products
- Payments connected to order totals

## Deliberate data-quality issues

- Duplicate customer entities
- Blank ZIP codes
- Inconsistent state formats
- Leading/trailing spaces
- Missing product categories
- Null discounts
- Negative quantities
- Mixed order-date formats
- Freight outliers

## Ethical use

The company and every record are synthetic. Present this as a portfolio simulation, not employment or confidential company data.
